About

We are not here to tell you what is in fashion.

Threadz is a small, independent guide to the practical side of clothes: finding a size that fits, building outfits you can repeat and packing without panic.

Trends change every season. A shirt that fits across the shoulders is useful for ten years.

What we publish

Two long guides and three tools, kept up to date. Every guide is written from experience, checked against standard garment measuring practice and read by a second person before it goes live.

What we do not

We do not sell clothes, promote labels or take payment for mentions. We do not comment on bodies. Sizes are measurements, and nothing more than that.

How to reach us

Corrections and questions are welcome on the contact page. Threadz is based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
